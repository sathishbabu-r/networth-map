# PRODUCT · My Money Huddle

## 1. THE JOB

Help a business owner go from:

> **“I have something financially important I keep putting off.”**

to:

> **“I know what I want to achieve and what I will do this week.”**

within **15 minutes or less**.

The user should feel like they are having a **Money Huddle**, not completing a financial form.

Success means:

**Confirmed Snapshot → One Goal → One Dated Action**

---

## 2. THE USER

The first user is **Ratheesh**, a business owner with income, assets, loans, expenses and family commitments spread across different places.

He may not know every number.

The product accepts:

- exact figures,
- estimates,
- ranges,
- “I don't know.”

He should complete the session independently.

---

## 3. THE HUDDLE EXPERIENCE

My Money Huddle is a **conversational interface**.

At every turn, Ratheesh can choose:

**🎙 Speak** or **⌨ Type**

The conversation begins:

> **Money Huddle:**  
> What is the one financial issue on your mind right now?

Ratheesh can type:

> I have a lot of customer payments outstanding and cash is becoming tight.

Or tap the microphone and say the same thing.

The app then asks **one relevant question at a time**.

> **Money Huddle:**  
> Roughly how much is outstanding from customers?

> **Ratheesh:**  
> Around eight lakh.

The app interprets the answer and proposes:

> **I understood:**  
> Customer receivables: ₹8,00,000  
> Estimate

**[✓ Confirm] [Edit]**

Once confirmed, the conversation continues.

> **Money Huddle:**  
> How much of this is overdue?

> **Ratheesh:**  
> Maybe five lakh.

> **I understood:**  
> Overdue receivables: ₹5,00,000  
> Estimate

**[✓ Confirm] [Edit]**

The conversation continues until the product has enough information to help with the issue.

It does **not** attempt to complete the entire Networth MAP.

---

## 4. FROM CONVERSATION TO ACTION

During the conversation, My Money Huddle progressively builds a structured financial snapshot behind the scenes.

The experience remains conversational.

When enough confirmed information exists, the product can surface a deterministic insight.

For example:

> **Money Huddle:**  
> You have ₹5 lakh overdue out of ₹8 lakh total receivables.  
> That's **62.5% currently overdue.**

Then:

> **What would you like to achieve?**

Ratheesh might say:

> I want to collect at least three lakh this month.

The app proposes:

> **Your Goal**  
> Collect ₹3,00,000 of outstanding receivables this month.

**[✓ Confirm] [Edit]**

Then:

> **Money Huddle:**  
> What's one action you can realistically take this week?

Ratheesh:

> I'll call my five biggest overdue customers.

The app asks:

> **When will you do it?**

Ratheesh:

> By Friday.

The app confirms:

> **THIS WEEK'S ACTION**  
> Call the five customers with the largest overdue balances by Friday.

**[Commit]**

---

## 5. HUDDLE COMPLETE

At the end of the conversation, show one simple summary:

# Your Money Huddle

**SNAPSHOT**  
₹8 lakh customer receivables  
₹5 lakh overdue

**INSIGHT**  
62.5% of receivables are overdue.

**GOAL**  
Collect ₹3 lakh this month.

**THIS WEEK**  
Call the five largest overdue customers by Friday.

Then:

> **Continue with My Money Huddle**  
> ₹999 for one month of Money Huddle check-ins.

**[Continue for ₹999]**

Payment happens inside the product.

---

## 6. RETURN HUDDLE

When Ratheesh returns, the product remembers confirmed context.

It does not restart the questionnaire.

Instead:

> **Money Huddle:**  
> Last time you committed to calling your five largest overdue customers by Friday.  
>
> **What happened?**

Ratheesh can **speak or type**.

For example:

> I called all five. Three of them paid me. I received two lakh.

The product proposes:

> **Action:** Called five customers  
> **Status:** Completed  
> **Amount collected:** ₹2,00,000

**[✓ Confirm] [Edit]**

The system keeps **action completion** separate from **financial outcome**.

Doing the work is not automatically counted as financial improvement.

---

## 7. V1 RULES & MILESTONES

### Interaction

The entire first session should feel like **one conversation**.

Every question supports:

**🎙 Speak | ⌨ Type**

Voice in v1 is **push-to-talk**.

The AI response can remain text.

Continuous listening and spoken AI responses wait.

### AI can

- ask relevant follow-up questions,
- understand spoken and typed answers,
- extract structured financial information,
- identify missing information,
- help clarify the user's goal,
- help make an action specific,
- remember confirmed context.

### Financial insight rule

> **Formula = allowed.  
> AI judgement = wait.**

Example allowed:

> “₹5 lakh of your ₹8 lakh receivables are overdue — 62.5%.”

Example not allowed:

> “Your receivables situation is unhealthy.”

### V1 includes

**Chat + Push-to-talk → Confirm → Snapshot → Formula Insight → Goal → Action → Return → Pay**

### V1 leaves out

- Full Networth MAP
- Bank connections
- Document uploads
- Portfolio recommendations
- AI financial judgement
- Continuous voice
- Spoken AI responses
- Multiple goals
- Complex dashboards
- Automated reminders
- Automated outcome verification
- Long-term reporting

### Milestone 1 — FINISH

Ratheesh independently completes:

**Snapshot + Goal + Dated Action**

within **15 minutes**.

### Milestone 2 — ACT

Ratheesh completes his chosen action within **7 days**.

### Milestone 3 — PAY

Ratheesh pays:

**₹999 for one month of Money Huddle check-ins**

inside the product.
